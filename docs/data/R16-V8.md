# Revision 16: renames scoped by name, recovery from a new-form glob (interface v8)

## Why

r15 learned bulk renames (probe 4 → 17/20) but its averaged model and seeds missed the declared promotion rule ([R15-V8](R15-V8.md)). Two of its three probe misses came from one habit: scoping `glob` with the *new* form of the names (`track-*.wav` for `track one.wav`), which matches nothing that changes. Every r15 spaces episode had scoped by extension only.

## Changes

- **Interface (v8, unreleased, amended):** when a glob-scoped `fs.rename` changes nothing, lilguy now says the glob matches current names, not new ones, and lists the files that would change but are outside the glob. Example: `nothing to rename: glob 'track-*.wav' matches current names in music, not new ones; it matches track-three.wav, already in that form; files that would change but are outside the glob: track one.wav, track two.wav`. The unscoped message is unchanged.
- **Data, revision 16 = revision 15 plus:**
  - `rename_spaces_prefix` (40): the request names a subset by its leading word, and the call scopes it with that word and the space kept (`glob: track *.wav`); another spaced file in the folder and, in half the episodes, an already-dashed file are left alone.
  - `rename_glob_recover` (30): the first call uses the new-form glob, lilguy's message explains, the next call uses the current form, then a bare `done`. The wrong first call is masked out of the training targets and kept only as context.
- **Probe fix:** `spaces_videos_clips` said "the .mp4 clips" but expected every `.mp4` renamed, including `intro take` and `outro take`; it now says "the .mp4 files".

No trainer or recipe change.

## Promotion rule, declared before training (Ivy, 2026-11-27)

Like for like, all on the RTX 3090 and the current roster:
- r16 seed mean at least r14's seed mean (67.67), with no seed below 62;
- r16 averaged model (fixed equal-weight soup of seeds 42/43/44) at least 3.0's 73/106;
- r16 averaged model's rename probe above 17/20 (r15's soup), with zero unreviewed effects and no partial batch.

This replaces r15's rule, which held each single seed to the score of 3.0's averaged model; r14's own seeds would have failed it too. A pass sends the averaged model to held-out v4, once, against 3.0; held-out v4 then decides, followed by the release safety gate. Same recipe as r15 (lr 1e-4, cosine, up to 2 epochs, seeds 42/43/44).

**Decided (Ivy, 2026-11-28): the r16 averaged model goes to held-out v4 anyway**, recorded as a change to the rule made *after* the development scores were seen. Reasons: the miss is one task in 106, within run-to-run noise; every seed and the probe passed; held-out v4 is the declared decider. Cost: a development rule overridden after scoring means the development numbers carry no promotion weight here; held-out v4 alone decides.

Declared before the run: held-out v4 runs once on the r16 soup (v8) and once on shipped 3.0 (v6), same GPU. The soup qualifies for release only if it scores above 3.0, with zero changes reaching a workspace without review; the paired gains and losses and the sign test are reported either way. Before any model ran, the suite's controls were re-run at v8 (scripted pass, immediate-`done` fail on every task), from their own test file: the frozen suite pins `tests/sandbox/test_holdout_suite_v4_controls_live.py` (interface 6), and a first attempt that edited it was refused by the freeze gate before any model ran.
